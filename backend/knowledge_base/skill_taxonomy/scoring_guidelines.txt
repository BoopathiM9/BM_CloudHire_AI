# RECRUITMENT SCORING GUIDELINES & FAIRNESS DIRECTIVES

## 1. 100-Point Scoring Weights
- Required Skills Match: 40 Points (Core mandatory technical competencies)
- Relevant Experience: 25 Points (Demonstrated years in relevant domain)
- Relevant Production Projects: 20 Points (Verified portfolio applications)
- Preferred Skills Match: 10 Points (Nice-to-have bonus competencies)
- Education & Industry Certifications: 5 Points (Accredited degree or active certifications like AWS Solutions Architect, CKA, etc.)

## 2. Fairness & Compliance Rules
- Zero Protected Attributes: Scoring must strictly exclude age, gender, ethnicity, race, religion, location, or photographs.
- Evidence Distinction: Distinguish clearly between a skill that is demonstrably missing vs. a skill simply not mentioned in the resume text.
- Human Review Support: Explanations must provide factual rationale to empower human recruiters, not make autonomous employment decisions.
